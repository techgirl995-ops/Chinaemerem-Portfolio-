Outreach Copywriting Samples

Sample sequences written to demonstrate structure and approach — not tied to a specific past client.

Cold Email Rewrite — Before & After

Before (generic, low-response version)
Subject: Boost your business with our software Hi there, I hope this email finds you well. My company offers a powerful HR software solution that can help businesses like yours save time and increase efficiency. We have helped many clients streamline their processes. Would you be interested in learning more? Let me know if you'd like to schedule a call. Thanks, Sales Team
After (targeted, personalized version)
Subject: Quick question about Nexora onboarding process Hi Nathan, Noticed Nexora has been growing fast, congrats. Scaling usually means HR ends up buried in manual onboarding: paperwork, time tracking, payroll setup, repeated across every new hire. We built PeopleFlow to take that off your plate. Teams like yours have cut manual HR admin by up to 60%, freeing up time for the parts of the job that actually need a human. Worth a 10-minute chat next week to see if it'd help Nexora too? Best regards, Chinaemerem

What changed: a specific trigger instead of a generic opener, one concrete proof point instead of vague claims, a named product tied to a real pain point, and a low-friction, specific ask.

3-Step Cold Email Sequence

Email 1 — Initial Outreach
Subject: Quick question about [Company] onboarding process Hi [Name], Noticed [Company] has been growing fast, congrats. Scaling usually means HR ends up buried in manual onboarding: paperwork, time tracking, payroll setup, repeated across every new hire. We built PeopleFlow to take that off your plate. Teams like yours have cut manual HR admin by up to 60%, freeing up time for the parts of the job that actually need a human. Worth a 10-minute chat next week to see if it'd help [Company] too?
Email 2 — Follow-Up (3 days later)
Subject: Re: Quick question about [Company] onboarding process Hi [Name], Following up in case this got buried, I know HR inboxes move fast. One thing teams tell us after switching to PeopleFlow: new hires are fully set up before their first day even starts, instead of scrambling through it in week one. Happy to show you exactly how it'd map onto your current process, 10 minutes, no pressure either way.
Email 3 — Breakup Email (7 days later)
Subject: Should I close the loop on this? Hi [Name], I don't want to keep cluttering your inbox, this'll be my last note on this. If cutting onboarding admin time isn't a priority right now, no worries at all. But if it ever becomes one, feel free to reach out directly and I'll pick this back up.

LinkedIn Outreach Sequence

Message 1 — Connection Request
Hi [Name], I came across your profile while researching ops leaders in logistics. Would love to connect and follow your work.
Message 2 — Follow-Up (2 days after connection accepted)
Thanks for connecting, [Name]. Curious how [Company] currently handles dispatch scheduling, is it still mostly manual, or have you moved to a routing tool? Always interested in how ops teams your size are solving this.
Message 3 — Meeting Ask (4 days later, if engaged)
Thanks for sharing that. We've been working with a few logistics teams on cutting dispatch planning time, and given what you mentioned about your setup, I think it could be a similar fit. Open to a quick 15-minute call this week to see if it's worth exploring?
